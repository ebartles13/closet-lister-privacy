Privacy Policy

Closet Lister is a personal tool for creating eBay listings from item photos. This policy explains what it does and does not do with your data.

What We Collect

  • Photos you upload. They are resized in your browser and sent to our server only to generate listing text. They are not stored after the listing is written.
  • Your eBay connection. If you connect eBay, we use encrypted authentication information to post listings on your behalf. We never see your eBay password.

What We Share

  • Photos and listing text are sent to Anthropic, the AI service that writes the listing, and when you choose to post to eBay, to eBay to create your listing.
  • We do not sell your data or share it with anyone else.

Your Control

  • You can disconnect eBay at any time, which deletes the stored token.
  • Clearing your browser cookies removes your saved eBay connection.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the account owner who operates this tool.
